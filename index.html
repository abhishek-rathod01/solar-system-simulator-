    No WebGL here

    This page renders the solar system on the GPU, and your browser isn't giving it a WebGL context. Nothing is broken on your end — it's usually one of three things.

    Hardware acceleration is switched off in browser settings; the browser is running in a software-only mode; or a very old GPU driver is being blocklisted.

    In Chrome, check chrome://gpu. In Firefox, check about:support under Graphics. Safari 15+ on macOS works without configuration.
